Table~\ref{tab:mesh_motion_settings} lists the fluid mesh motion of each case (Section~\ref{sec:fluid_mesh_motion}).
The pseudo-solid has a shear modulus $\mu^\Gamma = 1$ and a bulk modulus of $3$ (so $\lambda^\Gamma = 7/3$), in consistent units, in every case; only their ratio matters, as every boundary condition of the pseudo-solid prescribes a displacement.
